Our Story

Two friends, one old roaster, and a very stubborn idea.

Ember & Oak started in a converted print shop on Redchurch Street with a second-hand roaster and a promise: never serve a cup we wouldn't queue for ourselves.

Nine years later we still roast every batch in the back room, buy directly from farmers in Ethiopia, Colombia and Guatemala, and know most of our regulars by their usual order.

— Tom & Priya, founders
