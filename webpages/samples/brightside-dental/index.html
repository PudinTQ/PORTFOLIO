FAQ

Questions? We've got answers.

Do you accept my insurance?

We're in-network with most major PPO plans including Delta Dental, Cigna, Aetna and MetLife. Call us and we'll verify your benefits for free before your visit.

I don't have insurance. Can I still be seen?

Absolutely. Our Brightside Membership Plan costs $29/month and includes two cleanings, exams, X-rays and 20% off all other treatments.

What should I bring to my first appointment?

A photo ID, your insurance card (if any) and a list of medications. You can also fill out your forms online after booking to save time.

Do you offer sedation for anxious patients?

Yes. We offer nitrous oxide ("laughing gas") and oral sedation options so you can stay calm and comfortable throughout your treatment.
